Official logo files for repository use are in [docs/assets/](docs/assets/).

## What you may do without asking

- Use the name **FairLB** to refer to this software factually — in documentation,
  blog posts, talks, comparisons, and package descriptions.
- State that your product "works with FairLB", "is built on FairLB", or
  "is compatible with FairLB", as long as the statement is accurate.
- Redistribute unmodified builds under the name FairLB.

## What needs permission

- Naming a **modified** distribution "FairLB" or anything confusingly similar.
  Fork freely — just pick your own name for the fork, so users can tell whose
  build they are running.
- Using the FairLB name or logo in a way that implies endorsement, affiliation,
  or that your service *is* the official hosted offering.
- Registering domains, social accounts, or package names whose primary element
  is "FairLB".
