# Getting help

**Questions and "how do I…"** — open a
[discussion](https://github.com/fairlb/fairlb/discussions). Anything that turns
out to be a real answer usually ends up in the docs, so asking is useful even
when it feels obvious.

**Bugs** — open an [issue](https://github.com/fairlb/fairlb/issues/new/choose).
The template asks for the version and what you saw; both matter more than they
sound like they do, because provider behaviour varies and half of the surprising
reports come down to which upstream was involved.

**Security problems** — not here. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); that channel
is private.

**Commercial support** — FairLB Cloud is the hosted version and comes with a
support commitment. This repository is maintained on a best-effort basis: issues
get read, and there is no response-time promise attached to them.
