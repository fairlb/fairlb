# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

**In short:** be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion focused on
the work. Harassment, personal attacks, and demeaning remarks are not welcome
here — not in issues, not in pull requests, not in any project space.

Reports go to the contact address in `SECURITY.md`. Reports are handled
privately; the reporter's identity is not disclosed without their consent.
